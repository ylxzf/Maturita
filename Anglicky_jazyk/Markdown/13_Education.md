## My School (Technical School Jihlava)

### Building Description
- **Location**: Town centre
- **Structure**:
  - Three-storey building (two 100+ year old structures)
  - Modern interior adaptations
- **Facilities**:
  - Administration: Headmaster's office, secretariat
  - 20+ classrooms
  - Specialised rooms: Language labs, computer labs
  - Technical workshops
  - 3 gyms (including fitness)
  - Sports fields
  - School buffet (no canteen)

### Academic Year
- **Duration**: 1 Sept - 30 June
- **Terms**:
  - Autumn: Sept-Jan
  - Spring: Feb-Jun
- **Holidays**:
  - Autumn, Christmas, midyear, spring, Easter breaks
  - Summer holidays (July-August)
- **Assessment**: Term reports with grades

### Curriculum
- **Study Fields**:
  - IT & Web Design
  - Electrical Engineering
  - Mechanical Engineering
- **Core Subjects**:
  - Czech, English, Maths
  - Sciences (Physics, Chemistry)
  - Humanities (History, Civics)
  - PE
- **Technical Subjects**:
  - Electronics, Automation
  - Digital Technology
  - Engineering Mechanics

### School-Leaving Exam
- **Requirements**:
  - Czech language
  - Maths or English
  - Technical subjects
- **Outcome**: Maturita certificate upon success

## Czech Education System Overview

### School Types
| Type | Funding | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| State Schools | Free | Majority |
| Church Schools | Free | Religious affiliation |
| Private Schools | Paid | Alternative curricula |

### Educational Stages

1. **Pre-School**
   - Nursery (6 months+)
   - Kindergarten (3-6 years)

2. **Compulsory Education**
   - **Duration**: 9 years (ages 6-15)
   - **Subjects**:
     - Core academics
     - ICT basics
     - Physical education

3. **Secondary Education**
   - **Duration**: Typically 4 years
   - **Types**:
     - **Grammar Schools**:
       - Academic focus
       - Prepares for university
     - **Technical Schools**:
       - Specialised fields (IT, engineering)
       - Leads to employment or higher education
     - **Vocational Schools**:
       - Trade skills (hairdressing, construction)
       - Direct workforce entry

4. **Higher Education**
   - **Options**:
     - Universities (3-6 year degrees)
       - Example: Charles University (founded 1348)
     - Polytechnics (e.g., Jihlava Polytechnic)
     - Higher Vocational Schools (3-year programmes)
