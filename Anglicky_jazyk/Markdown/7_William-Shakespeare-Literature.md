## Biography
- **Born**: 1564, Stratford-upon-Avon
- **Family**:
  - Father: John Shakespeare (glove maker)
  - Married Anne Hathaway (18 years old, she was 26)
  - Children: 2 daughters, 1 son (died aged 11)
- **Career**:
  - 1592: Joined London acting troupe (later King's Men)
  - 1599: Bought Globe Theatre
  - Wrote plays under patronage of James I
- **Later Life**:
  - Returned to Stratford after Globe fire and son's death
  - Died 23 April 1616 (allegedly on his birthday)

## *Romeo and Juliet* Synopsis
- **Setting**: Verona, Italy
- **Plot**:
  1. Feud between Montagues and Capulets
  2. Secret love/marriage of Romeo and Juliet
  3. Romeo exiled after killing Juliet's cousin
  4. Juliet's fake death with poison
  5. Romeo's suicide → Juliet's real suicide
- **Outcome**: Families reconcile
