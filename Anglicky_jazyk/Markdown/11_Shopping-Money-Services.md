## Key Retail Concepts
- **Grocer's**: General food + household items (UK equivalent of supermarket)
- **Chemist's**: Pharmacy + toiletries (US: "drugstore")
- **Shopping Centres**:
  - Multiple shops under one roof
  - Often include hypermarkets, eateries, cinemas
  - Open long hours (pro/con for workers)
  - Environmental impact from increased car use
