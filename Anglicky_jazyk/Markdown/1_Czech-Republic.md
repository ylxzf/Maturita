## Location and Geography

- **Landlocked country** in Central Europe  
- **Capital city**: Prague  
- **Area**: ~80,000 km²  
- **Borders**: Germany (longest border), Poland, Slovakia, Austria  
- **Divided into 3 historical regions**:  
  - **Bohemia** (Prague)  
  - **Moravia** (Brno)  
  - **Silesia** (Ostrava)  
- **Regions**: 14 (e.g., Vysočina, Central Bohemian, South Moravian)  
- **Highest mountain**: Sněžka (1,603 m)  
- **Other mountains**: Šumava, Krušné hory, Giant Mountains (Krkonoše)  
- **Rivers**: Vltava, Elbe, Oder, Morava, etc.  
- **Lakes**: Rožmberk (largest one)

## Political System

- **Form of Government**: Parliamentary republic  
- **Head of State**: President (elected for a 5-year term)  
  - **Current President**: **Petr Pavel** (since 2023)  
- **Head of Government**: Prime Minister  
  - **Current Prime Minister**: **Petr Fiala** (since 2021)  
- **Government Branches**:  
  - **Legislative**: Parliament (Chamber of Deputies + Senate)  
    - **Chamber of Deputies**: 200 members (elected for 4 years)  
    - **Senate**: 81 members (6-year terms; one third elected every 2 years)  
  - **Executive**: President and Government (Prime Minister + Ministers)  
  - **Judicial**: Independent courts (e.g. Constitutional Court, Supreme Court)  
- **Elections**: Public, secret, direct, democratic, and universal  

---

## History

- Became an independent state on 1/1/1993 after splitting from Czechoslovakia  
  - First president was **Václav Havel**  
- Part of Czechoslovakia since 1918, following the end of World War I  
- Under Nazi occupation during World War II (1939–1945)  
- A communist regime was established in 1948, lasting until 1989  
- The Velvet Revolution in 1989 ended communism and led to democratic reforms
