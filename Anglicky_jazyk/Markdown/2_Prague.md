## Location and Geography  

- **Situated**: On both banks of the **Vltava River** in central **Bohemia**  
- **Population**: ~1.3 million (largest city in Czechia)  
- **Role**:  
  - **Capital of the Czech Republic** since 1993  
  - Seat of the **President**, **government**, and **Parliament**  
  - Major **industrial**, **business**, **cultural**, and **transport** hub  

---

## Historical Districts  

- **Oldest parts**:  
  - **Hradčany** (Prague Castle area)  
  - **Vyšehrad**  
  - **Old Town** (Staré Město)  
  - **New Town** (Nové Město)  
  - **Lesser Town** (Malá Strana)  
  - **Josefov** (old Jewish Quarter)  

---

## Key Monuments  

### Prague Castle  
- **Former seat** of Czech kings, now the **President's office**  
- **St. Vitus Cathedral**:  
  - **Gothic** style, founded by **Charles IV**, completed in 1929  
  - **Crypt**: Burial site of Czech kings/queens  
  - **Coronation jewels** stored here  
- Other sights:  
  - **Old Royal Palace** (Vladislaw Hall)  
  - **Golden Lane**  

### Lesser Town (Malá Strana)  
- **Baroque architecture**: St. Nicolas Church  
- **Charles Bridge**:  
  - Connects Lesser Town to Old Town  
  - **Baroque sculptures**, views of Prague Castle  

### Old Town  
- **Old Town Square**:  
  - **Old Town Hall** with **astronomical clock**  

### New Town  
- **Wenceslas Square**:  
  - **750m-long boulevard** with shops, restaurants, theaters  
  - **Statue of St. Wenceslas** (symbol of independence)  
  - **National Museum** nearby

## Modern Architecture  
- **Dancing House**:  
  - Towers resemble a "dancing couple"  
  - Houses offices, a restaurant, and a congress center  
- **Žižkov TV Tower**:  
  - **200m tall**, with panoramic views  
  - Features **10 climbing baby sculptures** by David Černý  
- **O2 Arena**:  
  - Multifunctional sports/concert venue
