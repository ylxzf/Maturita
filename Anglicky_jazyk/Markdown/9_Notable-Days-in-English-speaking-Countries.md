## United Kingdom

### February
- **14 Feb - St. Valentine's Day**  
  - Romantic gifts (flowers, chocolates)  
  - Greeting cards ("Valentines")  
  - Named after St. Valentine, a Christian martyr  

### March/April
- **Easter**  
  - **Traditions**:  
    - Egg decorating and hunts  
    - Easter Bunny delivers baskets  
    - Roast lamb, hot cross buns  

### October
- **31 Oct - Halloween**  
  - Celtic origins (scaring spirits)  
  - Pumpkin lanterns, costumes  
  - "Trick or treat" for sweets  

### November
- **5 Nov - Guy Fawkes Night**  
  - Commemorates failed 1605 Gunpowder Plot  
  - Bonfires, fireworks, burning "Guy" effigies  
- **11 Nov - Remembrance Day**  
  - Honours WWI soldiers and later conflicts  
  - Poppy wearing, 2-minute silence at 11am  
  - Cenotaph ceremony (London)  

### December
- **Christmas**  
  - **24 Dec**: Stockings for Father Christmas  
  - **25 Dec**: Presents, turkey dinner, Christmas pudding  
  - **26 Dec (Boxing Day)**: Sports, visiting friends  

- **31 Dec - New Year's Eve**  
  - Less emphasized than Christmas  
  - Scottish "Hogmanay": Singing "Auld Lang Syne"

## United States

### Shared with UK
- Valentine's Day, Easter, Halloween  
- **11 Nov - Veterans Day** (equivalent to Remembrance Day)  

### Unique Holidays
- **4 July - Independence Day**  
  - Celebrates 1776 Declaration of Independence  
  - Fireworks, parades  

- **4th Thu Nov - Thanksgiving**  
  - **Origins**: 1621 Pilgrim and Native American harvest feast  
  - **Traditions**:  
    - Family gatherings  
    - Turkey dinner with stuffing, pumpkin pie  
    - Black Friday shopping  
    - American football games
