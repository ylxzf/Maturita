## Visiting the Doctor

### GP Consultation Process
1. **Appointment**:
   - Attend during surgery hours
   - Wait in waiting room
2. **Initial Checks**:
   - Nurse reviews medical records
   - Checks insurance card
   - May take temperature
3. **Examination**:
   - Doctor inquires about symptoms
   - Physical checks:
     - Chest/throat examination
     - Lung/heart auscultation
     - Tonsil inspection
   - Possible tests:
     - Blood pressure
     - Pulse
     - Lab tests
4. **Treatment**:
   - Diagnosis
   - Prescription (tablets, ointments, etc.)
   - Pharmacy visit ("chemist's" in UK)
5. **Recovery**:
   - Bed rest
   - Medication schedule
   - Follow-up if needed

## Healthy Lifestyle

### Nutrition Guidelines
- **Do**:
  - 5 small meals daily
  - Vegetables, fruits, whole grains
  - Lean proteins
  - Adequate water intake
- **Avoid**:
  - Excess sugar/salt
  - Fatty/spicy foods
  - Energy drinks
  - Excessive alcohol

### Additional Health Practices
- Regular exercise
- Sufficient sleep
- Stress management
- Routine medical check-ups
- Smoke-free lifestyle
- Drug avoidance
