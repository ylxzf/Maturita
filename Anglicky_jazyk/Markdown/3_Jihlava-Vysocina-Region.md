## Location and Geography  

### Jihlava  
- **Situated**: In the **Bohemian-Moravian Highlands** (hilly, forested area with lakes)  
- **Population**: ~50,000  
- **Role**:  
  - Industrial/cultural center of Vysočina Region  
  - **Regional capital** of Vysočina  

### Vysočina Region  
- **Location**: Central Czech Republic  
- **Terrain**: Hilly (Bohemian-Moravian Highlands)  
  - **Highest points**:  
    - Žďárské vrchy area  
    - Javořice hill (Jihlavské vrchy)  
- **Climate**: Relatively cold (compared to rest of Czechia)  
- **Transport**: D1 motorway crosses the region

## Economy and Transport  

### Jihlava Industries  
- **Engineering**: Bosch, Automotive Lighting, Motorpal  
- **Electronics**: Tesla (microelectronics)  
- **Textiles**: Modeta Style (swimwear)  
- **Food**: Brewery, dairy plant, bakeries  

### Transport  
- **Road**: Near Prague–Brno motorway  
- **Rail**: Connections to Prague, Brno, Veselí nad Lužnicí  
- **Local transit**: Buses and trolleybuses  

### Shopping  
- **Hypermarkets** and department store (Prior)  
- **Shopping centres**:  
  - City Park  
  - Aventin (includes multiplex cinema)

## Sights and Architecture  

### Jihlava  
- **ZOO**: In Jihlávka river valley  
- **Catacombs**: 2nd longest in Czechia (after Znojmo)  
- **St. Jacob’s Church**: With outlook tower  
- **Masaryk Square**:  
  - One of Czechia’s largest squares  
  - Features:  
    - Baroque St. Ignatius’ Church  
    - 2 fountains  
    - Plague column  
    - Town Hall  
- **Gothic churches**: St. Jacob’s, Holy Cross, Virgin Mary  
- **Historical structures**: Virgin Mary’s Gate, town wall remnants  

### Vysočina UNESCO Sites  
1. **Zelená Hora Church** (Žďár n/S) – Santini architecture  
2. **Telč**: Historic town with castle and square  
3. **Třebíč**: Basilica + Jewish Quarter
