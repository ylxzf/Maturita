## Overview  
- **Capital** of UK/England  
- **Population**: ~9 million (largest in Europe)  
- **Location**: River Thames, SE England  
- **History**:  
  - Founded as Roman settlement  
  - Great Fire (1666)  
  - WWII damage (Battle of Britain)  
---
## Transport  
- **Iconic systems**:  
  - Red double-decker buses  
  - Black taxis  
  - London Underground (world's oldest)  
- **Major airport**: Heathrow  
---
## Key Landmarks  

### Government/Historic Sites  
- **Houses of Parliament**:  
  - Gothic revival (19th century)  
  - Westminster Hall (original surviving part)  
  - Big Ben clock tower  
- **Whitehall**: Government offices  
- **10 Downing Street**: PM's residence  
- **Westminster Abbey**:  
  - Coronation site  
  - Royal weddings  
  - Burials: monarchs, poets, artists  
---
### Religious/Cultural Sites  
- **St. Paul's Cathedral**:  
  - Renaissance dome by Christopher Wren  
  - Rebuilt after Great Fire (17th century)  
- **The City**:  
  - Roman-founded financial district  
  - Banks/offices  
---
### Royal/Tourist Attractions  
- **Tower of London**:  
  - Former palace/prison  
  - Houses Crown Jewels (guarded by Beefeaters)  
- **Tower Bridge**:  
  - 19th-century bascule bridge  
- **Buckingham Palace**:  
  - Royal residence  
  - Changing the Guard ceremony  
---
### Public Spaces  
- **Trafalgar Square**:  
  - Nelson's Column (Battle of Trafalgar, 1805)  
  - Christmas tree/New Year celebrations  
- **Hyde Park**:  
  - Largest park  
  - Speaker's Corner  
---
### Museums/Shopping  
- **Major institutions**:  
  - British Museum (world's largest)  
  - National Gallery (Trafalgar Square)  
- **Shopping areas**:  
  - Piccadilly Circus  
  - Regent Street  
  - Oxford Street
