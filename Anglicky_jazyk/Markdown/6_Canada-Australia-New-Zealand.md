## Canada

### Location and Geography
- **Location**: Northern North America (excluding Alaska)
- **Area**: 10 million km² (2nd largest globally)
- **Borders**: 
  - South: USA
  - Northwest: Alaska (USA)

### Water Features
- **Great Lakes** (shared with USA):
  - Lake Superior (largest)
  - Niagara Falls (between Erie and Ontario)
  - Others: Huron, Michigan
- **Other major lakes**: Great Bear, Great Slave, Winnipeg
- **Rivers**:
  - Longest: Mackenzie (flows to Arctic)
  - Most important: St. Lawrence

### Landscape and Climate
- **Western region**: Cordilleras mountains (Mount Logan: ~6,000m)
- **Eastern region**: Mostly lowlands
- **Climate zones**: Arctic (North) to temperate (South)

### Population
- **Total**: ~41 million (low density)
- **Distribution**: 90% uninhabited; most live near southern border
- **Ethnic groups**: 
  - Majority: European (British/French descent)
  - Minorities: Inuit, First Nations
- **Languages**: English, French (official)

### History
- **Colonial era**: Originally New France (17th century)
- **British rule**: Since 1763
- **Dominion status**: 1867 (first British dominion)

### Political System
- **Government**: Constitutional monarchy
- **Head of State**: King Charles III (represented by Governor-General)
- **Head of Government**: Prime Minister
- **Flag**: Red/white with maple leaf (adopted 1964)

### Administrative Divisions
- **10 provinces** (e.g. Quebec)
- **3 territories**
- **Capital**: Ottawa
- **Major cities**: Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver

## Australia

### Location and Geography
- **Location**: Southern hemisphere (Pacific/Indian Oceans)
- **Area**: 7.7 million km² (smallest continent)

### Landscape
- **Eastern coast**: Great Dividing Range, Australian Alps (Mt. Kosciusko: 2,200m)
- **Central region**: Deserts (Great Sandy, Gibson, Victoria)
- **Water features**:
  - Major rivers: Murray, Darling (seasonal drying)
  - Unique wildlife: kangaroo, koala, dingo

### Population
- **Total**: ~27 million (low density)
- **Distribution**: Mostly urban, southeast coast
- **Ethnic groups**: 
  - Majority: European (British descent)
  - Minorities: Asian, Aboriginal
- **Language**: Australian English

### History
- **Discovery**: Dutch (17th century)
- **Colonisation**: British penal colony (18th century)
- **Commonwealth**: Established 1901

### Political System
- **Government**: Constitutional monarchy
- **Head of State**: King Charles III (represented by Governor-General)
- **Head of Government**: Prime Minister

### Administrative Divisions
- **6 states**
- **2 territories** (including ACT)
- **Capital**: Canberra
- **Major cities**: Sydney, Melbourne

## New Zealand

### Location and Geography
- **Location**: South Pacific (two main islands + smaller ones)
- **Landscape**:
  - North Island: Active volcanoes
  - South Island: Southern Alps (Mt. Cook: 3,800m)
- **Unique wildlife**: Kiwi (flightless bird)

### Population
- **Total**: ~5 million
- **Ethnic groups**: 
  - Majority: European descent
  - Minority: Māori
- **Languages**: English, Māori

### Political System
- **Government**: Constitutional monarchy
- **Head of State**: King Charles III
- **Capital**: Wellington
- **Largest city**: Auckland (North Island)

### National Symbol
- **Nickname**: "Kiwis" (after the bird)
