## Types of Celebrations
- **Holiday** (*svátek*): Official day off work
- **Feast** (*svátek*): Celebration day
- **Name day** (*jmeniny*): Celebration of one's given name
---
## Major Celebrations
1. **Personal Occasions**:
   - Christenings
   - Birthdays
   - Weddings

2. **Christian Feasts**:
   - Christmas (pagan origins)
   - Easter (pagan origins)

## Bank Holidays

| Date | Name | Significance |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 Jan | **New Year's Day** | Commemorates Czech Republic's establishment (1993) |
| March/April | **Good Friday & Easter Sunday** | Christ's crucifixion and resurrection |
| Easter Monday | **Easter Monday** | Traditional whipping ritual, decorated eggs |
| 1 May | **Labour Day** | Honors 1886 Chicago workers' strike |
| 8 May | **Liberation Day** | End of WWII (1945) |
| 5 July | **St. Cyril & Methodius Day** | Byzantine missionaries who brought Christianity (863) |
| 6 July | **Jan Hus Day** | Reformation martyr burned at stake (1415) |
| 28 Sept | **St. Wenceslas Day** | Patron saint murdered by brother (~935) |
| 28 Oct | **Independence Day** | Founding of Czechoslovakia (1918) |
| 17 Nov | **Freedom & Democracy Day** | 1939 Nazi protests & 1989 Velvet Revolution |
| 24-26 Dec | **Christmas** | Christmas Eve, Day, and St. Stephen's Day |
---
## Easter Traditions
- **Food**: Yeast cakes, Easter lambs
- **Customs**:
  - Boys/men whip girls (receiving decorated eggs)
  - Alcohol often exchanged
