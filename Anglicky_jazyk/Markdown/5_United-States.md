## Location and Geography
- **Location**: Southern North America (includes Alaska and Hawaii)
- **Area**: 9 million km² (4th largest globally)
- **Borders**:
  - South: Mexico
  - North: Canada
  - West: Pacific Ocean
  - East: Atlantic Ocean
---
## Landscape
- **Western region**: Cordilleras mountains (occupies half of continental USA)
- **Highest peak**: Denali, Alaska (6,194m)
- **Eastern region**: Plains/Appalachian Highlands
- **Major water features**:
  - Rivers: Mississippi-Missouri system
  - Great Lakes (world's largest freshwater reservoir)
  - Niagara Falls (Lakes Ontario-Erie)
  - Grand Canyon (Colorado River)
---
## Climate
- **Varied zones**: Arctic (Alaska) to subtropical (Florida)
- **Coastal differences**: Atlantic vs. Pacific climates
- **Hawaii**: Tropical climate
---
## National Parks
- Notable examples: Yellowstone, Grand Canyon, Denali
---
## Population
- **Total**: ~330 million (75% urban)
- **Demographics**:
  - 80% White (including Czech diaspora)
  - Diverse racial/ethnic mix
- **Languages**:
  - Primary: American English
  - Minority: Spanish, Chinese etc.
---
## Language Notes
- **American vs. British English**:
  - Vocabulary: e.g. "drugstore" (UK: chemist's), "French fries" (UK: chips)
  - Simplified spelling: e.g. "program" (UK: programme), "center" (UK: centre)
---
## Political System
- **Federal republic**: 50 states (48 contiguous + Alaska + Hawaii)
- **Constitution**: Federal law supersedes state laws
- **Heads of government**:
  - State level: Governors
  - Federal level: President (Joe Biden, Democratic Party)
- **Legislature**: Congress (Washington D.C.)
- **Major parties**: Democratic, Republican
---
## Major Cities
- Capital: Washington D.C.
- Other key cities: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago
