## Housing: Town vs Country Living

### Urban Living
- **Advantages**:
  - Schools (including secondary/university)
  - Job opportunities
  - Public transport access
  - Shopping/cultural facilities
  - Healthcare proximity
- **Disadvantages**:
  - Traffic congestion
  - Noise pollution
  - Air pollution
  - Higher crime rates

### Rural Living
- **Advantages**:
  - Peaceful environment
  - Green spaces
  - Healthier air quality
  - Space for gardening/animals
- **Disadvantages**:
  - Limited schools (often primary only)
  - Fewer jobs
  - Poor public transport
  - Car dependency

## UK vs CZ Housing Comparison

### Czech Housing
- **Common Types**:
  - Prefabricated paneláks (socialist-era)
  - Apartment blocks
  - Increasing detached houses
- **Ownership**: Mix of owned/rented

### Terminology
- **Czech "dům"**: Broad term (villas, offices, schools)
- **English "house"**: Single-family dwelling only

### UK Housing Trends
- **Prevalence**: 7/8 live in houses (vs 1/8 in flats)
- **Typical British House**:
  - Two floors + gardens (front/back)
  - **Ground floor**: Hall, kitchen, living/dining
  - **First floor**: Bedrooms, bathroom
  - **Differences from CZ**:
    - Smaller footprint
    - No cellar
    - Combined toilet/bathroom
