## Key Concepts
- **Work**: Any mental/physical activity (paid or unpaid)
- **Job**: Paid employment position

## Salary Landscape (CZ)
- **High-paying**: Managers, IT specialists, private doctors
- **Low-paying**: Cleaners, postal workers, retail staff
---
## Ideal Job Considerations
- **Compensation**: Salary, bonuses, benefits
- **Content**: Responsibilities, interest level
- **Conditions**: 
  - Hours (shifts, overtime, flexibility)
  - Leave (holiday, sick pay)
  - Location (commute, remote options)

## Application Process
### Required Documents
- **CV** (Curriculum Vitae)
- **Cover Letter** containing:
  - Position reference
  - Relevant experience
  - Availability for interview

### Interview Tips
**Do**:
- Research the company
- Arrive early
- Dress appropriately
- Prepare questions
- Highlight relevant skills

**Don't**:
- Criticise past employers
- Exaggerate qualifications
- Interrupt interviewers
