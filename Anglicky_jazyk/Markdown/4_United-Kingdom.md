## Location and Geography  
- **Official name**: United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland  
- **Composition**:  
  - 2 main islands: Great Britain + Northern Ireland  
  - ~5,000 smaller islands (Channel Islands, Isle of Man, Hebrides, Orkneys, Shetlands)  
- **Area**: 245,000 km² (~3× larger than Czechia)  
- **Neighbours**: Ireland, France  
---
## Landscape  
- **Lowlands**: Primarily in England  
- **Highlands**:  
  - Northern England (Lake District)  
  - Wales  
  - Scotland (Ben Nevis - UK's highest peak at 1,342m)  
- **Lakes ("lochs" in Scotland)**:  
  - Loch Lomond (largest)  
  - Loch Ness (famous for "monster")  
- **Major rivers**: Severn, Thames  
---
## Climate  
- **Mild** with small seasonal temperature variation  
- **Cooler summers, warmer winters** vs. Czechia  
- **Frequent rainfall** year-round  
---
## People and Culture  
- **Population**: ~70 million (mostly urban, high density in South England)  
- **Languages**:  
  - English (main)  
  - Celtic minority languages: Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Irish Gaelic  
---
## Economy  
- **Mineral resources**: Coal, tin, gas, oil, iron  
- **Highly developed** industry/agriculture  
- **80% workforce** in services  
---
## Political Structure  
- **Constitutional monarchy** (Head of State: King Charles III)  
- **4 constituent countries**:  
  - England (Capital: London)  
  - Wales (Cardiff)  
  - Scotland (Edinburgh)  
  - Northern Ireland (Belfast)  
- **Government**:  
  - Prime Minister (Rishi Sunak, Conservative Party)  
  - Parliament: House of Commons + House of Lords  
---
## International Relations  
- **Member**: UN, NATO  
- **Former EU member** (left via Brexit in 2016)  
- **Currency**: Pound sterling (£)
